# L9 – Laser Cut 

## Design - 

When beginning this project my partner and I began by measuring the wood that we were given from three different points. When measuring 
we got values of 1.010,0.010, 0.010, we got an average value of 0.010 inches for our piece of wood, as shown below by the pictures of 
the measuring values. The value of 0.010 would now end up being our thickness variable in the final cut. 

__Kerf measurement__ 

When putting in a 12 by 12 mm square into the laser cutter, we got a final kerf measurement of 0.02
